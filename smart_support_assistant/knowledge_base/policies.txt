📑 Sample Support Policies
Refund Policy
Customers can request a refund within 7 days of purchase.

Refunds are processed within 5 business days.

Refunds are not applicable for digital products once downloaded.

Shipping Policy
Standard shipping takes 5–7 business days.

Express shipping is available at extra cost and takes 2–3 business days.

Tracking numbers are provided once the order is dispatched.

Cancellation Policy
Orders can be cancelled within 24 hours of placement.

After 24 hours, cancellation requests may not be accepted if the order has already shipped.

Exchange Policy
Exchanges are allowed within 14 days of delivery.

Items must be unused and returned in original packaging.

Exchanges are subject to stock availability.

Complaint & Escalation
Legal or sensitive complaints are escalated to the human support team.

Customers can raise complaints via chat, email, or phone.

Escalated issues are handled within 48 hours by a dedicated support agent.
